Why “tŷ”?

A little roof over every letter

Tŷ is the Welsh word for house — and in Welsh, the accent over the ŷ is called a to bach: literally, a little roof. We liked that. It's what we do — we put good roofs over students' heads, and we sweat the little things underneath them.

We're not a chain and we're not a call centre. We're local, Rent Smart Wales accredited, and every house we let is one we'd be happy to live in ourselves.

Good to know

Questions students actually ask

When should we start looking for next year?

In Bangor, most groups sort their house between November and February for the following September. The bigger houses go first — if you're a group of 6+, message us early in the autumn term.

Are bills included?

It varies by house — each listing states exactly what's included. Where bills aren't included, we'll give you honest, realistic estimates from previous tenants rather than best-case numbers.

Do we all need guarantors?

Usually each tenant provides a UK-based guarantor. If that's difficult — for example for international students — talk to us and we'll work out an alternative such as advance payment options.

What happens when something breaks?

Message the same number you've always used — 07557 871 220. Urgent problems (heating, water, security) are dealt with as emergencies; everything else is usually sorted within a few days.

Are you accredited?

Yes — TY Lettings Ltd is registered and accredited with Rent Smart Wales, the statutory licensing scheme for landlords and agents in Wales. Your deposit is protected in a government-approved scheme.
